# S1 preregistration

Sprint S1, the routing benchmark (hub #302). This file is committed before any question is written and before any model
call, and each phase adds its section before the step it governs. P1 writes **Inputs**: the composition first, in the
commit before any inventory is rebuilt, then the inventories' hashes and sources in its last commit. P2 appends the
questions' hashes; P3 writes the rest before G2. A run's files go under `benchmark/runs/<date>/`, and this file names them.

## Inputs (P1)

### The composition

Every Socrata, CKAN and registry selection takes `active` entries only (the seat's ruling, hub #302 comment
6610180295).

| Size | Entries, by source |
|---|---|
| 50 | the 11 real servers (today's seven and R5's four); the 34 largest active US Socrata domains, as portal packs; 3 near-duplicates and 2 wrapper packs |
| 200 | the 50; the remaining 45 of the 79 active US Socrata domains with 50 or more datasets; the 70 largest of the other 138 active US Socrata domains; the 20 largest active US CKAN portals, as packs; 10 MCP Registry entries; 3 more near-duplicates and 2 more wrapper packs: 50 + 45 + 70 + 20 + 10 + 5 = 200 |
| 1,000 | the 200; the 95 remaining active Socrata domains that carry a country; the 400 largest active CKAN portals not already in, any country; 240 more MCP Registry entries; and, by the remainder rule, 1,000 − (200 + 95 + 400 + 240) = 65 near-duplicates |

The 400 CKAN portals and 240 registry entries at 1,000 are a design choice, not a measured share of any real directory.

"Largest" is by `dataset_count`, descending, ties broken by the catalog's `id` field ascending (on 7 Socrata and 50
CKAN rows the `id` is not the host in the row's `url`). Every other choice (the registry entries, the entries a
near-duplicate copies, the servers a wrapper wraps, each file's order) is in the order of the SHA-256 of the seed, a
purpose and a key; the seed is `civic-ai-tools S1 P1 inventories, 2026-11-03`. The composition is stated in
`benchmark/inventories/build.mjs` (`COMPOSITION`) and `benchmark/README.md`.

workspace.

**The registry fetch.** 567 requests, every one HTTP 200, from `2026-11-03T01:30:02Z` to `2026-11-03T01:42:17Z`. The
list (`version=latest`, 100 a page, following the cursor to its end) is 555 pages and 55,497 entries. The pool: an
entry whose name or description holds "open data" (or "open-data", "open_data", "opendata"), "government(s)" or
"city"/"cities" as a whole word bounded by non-letters, in any case; whose status is `active`; and which lists at least
one remote: 638 entries. An earlier run, capped at 100 pages by the script's first version, is not used.
